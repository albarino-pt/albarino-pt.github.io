A complete web platform for a professional photographer: a public marketing site plus a custom admin system for running the day-to-day business — client galleries, portfolio, and online print sales.

The public site presents the photographer's portfolio and client testimonials, with a contact form for new bookings. Clients get their own private event galleries, gated behind an access code, with configurable expiration dates.

The admin panel covers the full operational side: creating and managing events/galleries (with bulk image upload), a portfolio CMS for featured case studies, pricing configuration (print sizes, shipping, pickup options, pay-later vs. Stripe), and an orders dashboard tracking real sales and best-selling products.
